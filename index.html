What it watches

Every signal you already generate, in one place

Ten integrations feed one timeline. Each one is opt-in, read-only where it counts, and yours to disconnect.

Unified activity timeline

Every event from every connected source, merged into one searchable, filterable chronological feed.

Google account integration

Gmail security alerts, Calendar events and Drive files — read-only metadata, never message bodies.

GitHub activity

Pushes, pull requests, issues and stars — your public events by default, private ones only if you add a token.

ActivityWatch

Reads the time-tracking data ActivityWatch already collects locally — through its own API.

Wazuh SIEM

Bring your home-network security alerts into the same timeline as everything else.

Android / iOS companion

Device telemetry from your phone: battery, network, location — only when you grant OS permission.

Google Takeout importer

Backfill years of history: upload your Takeout archive and its entries land on the same timeline.

Detection rules & alerts

Declarative rules over your event stream — unusual sign-ins, dead agents, anything you define.

Insights & analytics

Heatmaps, streaks and correlations across sources — patterns no single app could show you.

Sessions & API keys

Every device and token is a named session you can inspect and revoke — individually, from Settings.

How privacy works

There is no GHOSTTRACE server to trust

GHOSTTRACE is software you run, not a service you sign up for. Every byte lives on hardware you control.

Runs on your hardware

One docker compose up on your own machine or homelab. There is no company server, no cloud sync, no phone-home. The integrations talk to their APIs from your instance, under your keys.

Minimal, scoped reads

OAuth scopes are read-only and gated by explicit consent. Gmail access reads security-alert subjects and dates only — never message bodies, never everything else in your mailbox.

Deletion is real

Deleting your account from Settings revokes the Google tokens and permanently cascades deletion of every collected event. Retention is configurable — default 90 days, not forever.
