2. Authorized use only

GHOSTTRACE aggregates activity from accounts, devices and services. You may use it only on accounts and devices that you own or are explicitly authorized to monitor.

That means, among other things: connect only your own Google account, your own GitHub account, your own ActivityWatch and Wazuh installations, and devices whose companion apps you have installed with permission.
